### Typography
- **Primary Typeface - Sans Serif Font**: A sans serif font aligns well with a brand that values clarity, modernity, and simplicity. It’s also highly legible, which is crucial for scientific and economic texts. A font like Helvetica or Arial could be ideal for body texts, while a more distinct sans serif font can be used for headings to add character.
- **Secondary Typeface**: For contrast and emphasis, a secondary typeface, still within the sans-serif family, but with slightly distinct features (like a different weight or a more rounded appearance) can be used for callouts or highlighted information.

### Visual Style
- **Scientific and Mathematical Imagery**: Use visuals that resonate with scientific and mathematical themes. Graphs, abstract representations of data, and minimalist iconography can be incorporated into design elements.
- **Elegant and Simple Layouts**: The overall layout for both the website and documents should be clean and uncluttered. Use white space effectively to create an elegant and sophisticated look. The focus should be on ease of navigation and content readability.

### Logo Design

- **Consistent Theming**: Ensure all documents follow the same style guide in terms of font, color scheme, and visual elements.

### Marketing and Outreach Materials

- **Social Media**: For platforms like LinkedIn, the content should maintain the brand’s serious and educational tone. Visual content should be sleek, simple, and align with the overall brand identity.
