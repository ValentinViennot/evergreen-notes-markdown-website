**Reporting** in the [[Smash Protocol]] allows users to flag inappropriate behavior or content within [[Smash Neighborhoods (NBH)]]. Since everything in Smash is encrypted, the reporting user will transmit the reported user’s data in a re-encrypted form to the [[Neighborhood Admin Bot (NAB)]], enabling the admin to investigate and take action. 

- Reports are handled at the neighborhood level, with the [[Smash Neighborhood Admins]] responsible for reviewing and acting on the reports.
- The process is decentralized, meaning each neighborhood has its own policies for handling reports.
- The decision to block the other peer is up to the blocking user.
