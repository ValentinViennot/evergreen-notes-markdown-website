**A free, safe, private, and Inclusive 1:N social protocol for quality connections.**

- Smash is a user-centered product concept.
- The Smash apps are powered by the [[Smash Protocol]].
- [[Smashchats]] is a proposed client app implementation of Smash.

With Smash, we aim to take power away from centralized, monopolized platforms and return it to the users.

The true value of social networks and platforms lies in two key assets:
- Their users' data (YOUR data)
- Their users graph (relations between users)

Unfortunately, centralizing technology and data also centralizes power—and power centralization inevitably leads to misuse and abuse.

Smash offers a solution: an interoperable, decentralized, distributed, private protocol that can power any social messaging application on the Internet, thus putting users in control by giving them full ownership of their data. Unlike existing platforms, **no single entity can ever ban users from their social lives and identities**.

The Smash project operates on three key levels:
- **A social messaging protocol**: The [[Smash Protocol]].
- **Smash [[Smash Neighborhoods (NBH)]]s**: Decentralized servers.
- **A user-friendly client app**: [[Smashchats]].

Smash introduces new concepts that enable an endless variety of interoperable social apps, allowing users to retain control of a sovereign ubiquitous identity and over their data across the Internet.
