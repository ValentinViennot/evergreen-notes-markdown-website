4. **Peer-to-Peer Communication**: Users can communicate directly without relying on a central server, preserving privacy and autonomy.

5. **No Single Point of Failure**: The decentralized nature of the protocol means there is no central authority or server that could be shut down or compromised.

8. **Privacy by Design**: The protocol ensures minimal data is shared, and only necessary information is disclosed during interactions.

9. **Scalable Social Graph**: Users can build and manage their social networks privately, with only trusted peers knowing about their connections.

10. **Anonymous Participation**: Users can participate in [[Smash Neighborhoods (NBH)]] and communications without revealing their real identity, preserving anonymity when needed.

11. **Inter-Neighborhood Communication**: Users can connect and communicate across different [[Smash Neighborhoods (NBH)]], maintaining their identity and relationships.

12. **Data Portability**: Users can easily move their data and connections across [[Smash Neighborhoods (NBH)]], ensuring freedom of movement without losing control of their information.

15. **No Central Authority**: The system operates without any central point of control, empowering users and preventing misuse of centralized power.

16. **Transparency and Consent**: Users are always informed about how their data is being used and can give or withhold consent at any time.

17. **Resilience and Reliability**: If peer-to-peer connections fail, the protocol provides fallback mechanisms to ensure uninterrupted communication.

18. **Community Governance**: Each neighborhood operates autonomously with its own rules, giving users the freedom to choose communities that match their values.

19. **Self-Regulation**: Users are responsible for curating their own experiences, choosing whom to trust and avoiding bad actors without relying on a central authority for moderation.

20. **Minimal Disclosure**: During interactions, only the minimum required data is shared, ensuring that users maintain control over their sensitive information.
