
**Smash or Pass: Building a Free Internet for Communities**

A safe, private, and inclusive 1:N social app for meaningful connections.

### **What is Smashchats?**

Smashchats is an open-source, decentralized, and private social messaging app built for communities. Fully distributed, it ensures users have complete control over their data, privacy, and social interactions.

### **Why Smashchats?**

- **Privacy and Control**: Unlike centralized apps, Smashchats gives you full ownership of your data. You decide who to share with and when.
- **Decentralized Architecture**: Smashchats is fully decentralized and distributed, meaning no single point of failure and no platform can ban you or take control of your identity.
- **Social Features**: Discover and connect with others in your communities, all without sacrificing your privacy.

### **Mission**
Reinvent the web for communities with a focus on safety, privacy, quality, and inclusivity.

### **Key Features**
- Free and open-source
- Fully decentralized and distributed
- End-to-end encryption for all communications
- Modular design for customization

---

### **Your Identity**
Your identity is protected by asymmetric encryption, with no need for passwords. Your encryption keys are generated locally on your device, and you can create multiple identities, manage your reputation, and back up your data as you choose.

### **Your Data**
Smashchats doesn’t store your data on servers—it doesn’t need them to operate. Data and communications are fully encrypted, exchanged peer-to-peer, and automatically deleted after 7 days unless you choose to save them. Your data is always directly exchanged between you and other users.

### **Your Community**
Smash is built around **Neighborhoods**—community-driven spaces where you connect with people who share your interests. Like real-world neighborhoods, there are good and bad ones. Stay safe, and share recommendations. You can contribute to, build, or fund your own Neighborhood.

---

### **Technology**

Smash doesn’t reinvent the wheel. Instead, it combines proven protocols to deliver a seamless, private experience with modular, micro-component architecture.

- **Identity**: AT Proto DID.
- **User Discovery**: Smash-Pass User Graph.
- **Messaging**: Smash Message Format.
- **Encryption**: Signal protocol, X3DH, and 2key-ratchet DH.
- **Message Delivery**: WebRTC or through TURN servers.
- **Content Distribution**: IPFS.
- **Trust Model**: PGP-style network.

---

### **Core Concepts**

**Identity**:  
Smashchats uses decentralized identifiers (DIDs) to establish secure identities. These can be reused across applications and linked to a DNS handle or username, providing both trust and privacy.

**Neighborhoods**:  
Community-driven nodes, Neighborhoods function like forums or servers. Users join using their DID and gain access to community-specific features, tools, and resilience.

**Badges**:  
Cryptographic assertions (e.g., age verification, group membership) signed by trusted peers. Badges can be part of user profiles to establish credibility or unlock certain features.

**Trust**:  
Smashchats uses a decentralized trust model built through peer interactions. Trust chains are formed by validating and endorsing DIDs, ensuring reliable and secure relationships without a central authority.

**Smash (or Pass)**:  
Mark someone as a favorite ("Smash") or opt-out of interaction ("Pass"). This categorizes connections, building trust layers from strangers to trusted contacts. Neighborhoods can build user graphs to make better recommendations based on your interactions, but they won’t see your private data or messages.

**Messages**:  
Smash supports 1:N messaging, meaning a single user can send messages to one or multiple users securely. Messages could include text, media, badges, or even content references. All messaging is peer-to-peer and encrypted, ensuring privacy.

**Content**:  
Using IPFS, Smashchats enables decentralized content distribution, empowering creators to share directly with their audience, free from centralized platforms.
