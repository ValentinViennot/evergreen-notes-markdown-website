Bluesky is a decentralized social media platform designed as an alternative to traditional, centralized networks like Twitter. Initially envisioned by Jack Dorsey while he was CEO of Twitter, Bluesky operates independently today under its own company structure, driven by the mission to create a more open, user-centric social experience. It is built on **[[ATProto]]**, which underpins its unique capabilities and focus on decentralization.

At its core, Bluesky feels familiar to users of platforms like Twitter, offering similar features such as posting, liking, and replying to content. However, the key difference lies in its decentralized architecture. Instead of being controlled by a single company, Bluesky allows users to retain control of their identity and content, offering **account portability**—meaning you can move to another server without losing your data or followers. This reduces platform lock-in, ensuring that users have greater autonomy over their digital presence.

Bluesky also embraces community-driven moderation, allowing server administrators to set their own content policies while giving users additional tools, such as blocklists, to manage their experience. The platform encourages a more customizable social feed experience, with options for users to create and follow algorithmic feeds tailored to their preferences.

Bluesky’s unique selling point is its commitment to **open standards**, privacy, and decentralization, giving users more freedom compared to centralized platforms like X (formerly Twitter). It fosters a playful, less promotional atmosphere, where users are encouraged to engage with content organically rather than through ads or aggressive algorithms.
