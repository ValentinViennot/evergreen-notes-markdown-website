The **Passing** action in the [[Smash Protocol]] represents a deliberate decision to reduce further interactions with another [[Peer Identity]]. It is a way for [[Peer]]s to mute someone, without informing the other peer directly.

When a user "passes" on another, the relationship remains at a lower level in the [[Trust Levels]] hierarchy. No further automated interactions will occur and the client UI may display the other peer as 'unknown' or 'muted'. However, the 'passing' user may still manually exchange data—such as messages—with the passed peer (as opposed to [[Blocking]]). 

This action may be shared with and processed by the associated [[Neighborhood Admin Bot (NAB)]], which helps curate the user’s experience in the neighborhood, such as refining recommendations or reducing visibility of the passed user.
