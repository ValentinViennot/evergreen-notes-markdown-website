**Smash Neighborhood Admins** are the individuals or organizations responsible for managing and administrating [[Smash Neighborhoods (NBH)]]. These admins take on the role of overseeing the infrastructure, governance, and user experience within their neighborhood. While the technical execution of administrative actions often falls to the automated [[Neighborhood Admin Bot (NAB)]], the human admins behind the neighborhood are the ones making key decisions.

1. **Governance and Policy Setting**: Smash Neighborhood Admins define the rules and culture of their community. They decide on acceptable behavior, content moderation policies, and membership criteria. They can also determine how their neighborhood integrates with other neighborhoods and peers ([[Web of Trust]]).
   
2. **Infrastructure Management**: Admins are responsible for deploying and maintaining the infrastructure required to run their neighborhood. This can include hosting servers, deploying [[Messaging Endpoints]], and ensuring the required relevant [[Core components (Infrastructure)]] of the [[Smash Protocol]] are in place.

3. **Trust and Reputation Management**: Admins help build the trustworthiness of the neighborhood by verifying and validating users. Through their [[Neighborhood Admin Bot (NAB)]], they may issue [[Badges]] or other reputation markers, fostering a web of trust that enhances the social experience within the neighborhood.

4. **User Interaction and Support**: While the [[Neighborhood Admin Bot (NAB)]] handles the automated processes of user management, the human admins are often responsible for resolving disputes, handling [[Reporting|reports]] (e.g., user abuse, [[GDPR]] requests), and providing support to users who interact within the neighborhood.

5. **Legal and Compliance**: Neighborhood admins are accountable for ensuring that their community complies with legal obligations. This can include enforcing GDPR-related processes, handling abuse reports, and ensuring that the neighborhood does not violate local regulations. The level of legal compliance can vary by neighborhood, depending on the admins' policies.

6. **Customizing and Enhancing the User Experience**: Admins have the flexibility to customize and extend the functionality of their neighborhood. They can implement unique features or services based on the [[Smash Protocol]] or integrate other decentralized technologies to tailor the user experience according to their community’s needs.

Smash Neighborhood Admins enjoy significant autonomy in how they run their communities. Each neighborhood can choose its own governance model—ranging from highly democratic to authoritarian, depending on the admin's vision. Admins can also choose whether their neighborhood is open, closed, or semi-open, controlling who can join and what kind of interactions are allowed.

Ultimately, the success and reputation of a neighborhood often depend on the active involvement and responsible decision-making of its human administrators. They ensure that the neighborhood remains a place where users can feel safe, engaged, and empowered while adhering to the decentralized ethos of the [[Smash Protocol]] (read [[Smash Values]] and [[Smash Principles]]).
