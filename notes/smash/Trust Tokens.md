**Trust Tokens** are a special type of **[[Endorsements]]** that signifies a peer’s trust in another peer's **[[Peer Identity]] (DID)**. A Trust Token represents an explicit statement by one **[[Peer]]** that they trust the other peer’s identity, not just in relation to specific claims or badges, but as a whole.

Unlike **[[Badges]] [[Endorsements]]**, which validate individual claims or achievements, Trust Tokens indicate that a peer trusts the identity and overall behavior of another peer within the context of the entire **Smash ecosystem**.

- **Selective Trust**: As with all Endorsements in Smash, peers have control over which Trust Tokens they display publicly. This allows users to curate their public profile while maintaining privacy in sensitive contexts.

Trust Tokens can carry more weight when issued by well-established and **trusted peers**. The more trusted the issuing peer, the more significant the Trust Token becomes in the eyes of others.  For example, a Trust Token issued by a **[[Neighborhood Admin Bot (NAB)]]** could be highly valued by other users in the same neighborhood, as it implies a higher level of verification and trust.

Trust Tokens are the fundamental building block of Smash's **[[Web of Trust]]**. By issuing Trust Tokens, peers contribute to a decentralized trust model where users' reputations are built organically through peer-to-peer endorsements, rather than relying on centralized authorities or algorithms.
