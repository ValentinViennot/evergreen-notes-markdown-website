**Reinventing the Free Web for Communities**

Our _freedom_ is at risk.

Every day, we edge closer to the end of the *free* internet. What was once a space for creativity, diversity, and _freedom_ is now a battleground for tech monopolies, with users caught in the crossfire.

We, the users, have become serfs in a digital feudal system.
We must pay our lords with our **data** and our **identities**.
We aren't _free_ to use our land as we wish.

The unreasonable fights between platforms has led to their [enshittification](https://pluralistic.net/2023/11/03/subprime-attention-rent-crisis/): the degradation of the very services we now depend on for our social lives, innovation, and consumption—all driven by the profit motives of a few tech giants.

**We deserve better software.**

Technology, especially the internet, has the potential to liberate us from these toxic, monopolistic platforms. But to achieve this, we must organize competitive and compelling product alternatives.

**This is what Smash is about.**

The [[Smash Protocol]] is about creating and utilizing web protocols to build _free_, decentralized, social apps that are about their end users.

Today, nearly every digital application is a social app. Due to the network effect and the difficulty of using a shared identity and data across applications, the biggest platforms hold us hostages. As a result, there is no real, [[free competition]] between them, and we’re forced to accept the freedom cost due to the lack of viable alternatives.
