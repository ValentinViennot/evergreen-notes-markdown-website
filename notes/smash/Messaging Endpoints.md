**Messaging Endpoints** in the [[Smash Protocol]] are relay servers responsible for handling [[signaling]] session data between [[Peer]]s and managing asynchronous messaging. 

They serve as **untrusted** intermediaries to ensure smooth communication when peers are not directly connected (offline or establishing a new P2P connection).

Authorized Messaging Endpoints types under the Smash Protocol include:

- **[[Smash Messaging Endpoint (SMEv1)]]: The current standard for managing both signaling and message relay within the network, ensuring quick and secure message exchange between peers.
