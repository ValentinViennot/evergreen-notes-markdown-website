**Blocking** in the [[Smash Protocol]] is a stronger action than [[Passing]], designed to completely eliminate any interaction between two [[Peer|peers]]. When a user blocks another, the blocked peer is entirely hidden and ignored by the blocking user’s client. This ensures that no further communication—**automated or manual**—can occur.

Blocking involves additional client-side configuration to ensure that the blocked peer is no longer visible in the user interface and that all interactions, including messaging, are entirely forbidden.

Like [[Passing]], the **Blocking** action may be communicated to the associated [[Neighborhood Admin Bot (NAB)]], enabling the neighborhood to adjust its recommendations and hide the blocked peer from the blocking user's experience.
