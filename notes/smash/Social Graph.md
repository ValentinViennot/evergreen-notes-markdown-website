The **social graph** represents the **relationships between all users** within the network. Smash defines two primary relationship types: [[Smash or Pass]].

A [[Peer]]'s **social local graph** is local to their client (see [[User actions are local]]). Each user is solely responsible for storing their entire social graph. A User's **social local graph** is a subset of the entire Smash network social graph, relative to them.

However, when [[Joining a Neighborhood]], a user:
- **(By default)**: Will share their future [[Smashing]] and [[Passing]] actions with the [[Neighborhood Admin Bot (NAB)]].
- **(Optional, opt-out)**: May choose to share their existing **social local graph** with the [[Neighborhood Admin Bot (NAB)]].
- **(Optional, default config / opt-out)**: May share all their **received** [[Trust Tokens]] with the [[Neighborhood Admin Bot (NAB)]] (as any [[trusted peer]]—[[default config]]).
