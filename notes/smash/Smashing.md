The Smashing action in the [[Smash Protocol]] refers to "**adding to favorites**."

It allows [[Peer]]s to save the contact information of another [[Peer Identity]] and is considered an upgrade in [[Trust Levels]]. The 'smashed' peer will receive more information from the 'smashing' peer.

This action is not communicated to the smashed peer but is typically shared with and processed by any associated [[Neighborhood Admin Bot (NAB)]].
